\section{Strengths and limitations of IRASim}
\label{sec:analysis}

\paragraph{Strengths.}
\textit{S1, the robot follows the actions:} the EVID error of Frame-Ada is only 0.32\,cm above that of the real videos.
\textit{S2, per-frame conditioning helps:} with the same backbone, Frame-Ada beats Video-Ada on every metric.
\textit{S3, stable:} its seeds do not differ significantly, and a rerun with the same batches reproduces the videos exactly.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\textit{L1, objects lose their identity,} typically once grasped (\cref{fig:strips}b).
\textit{L2, correct motion does not imply the correct outcome:} only 51\% and 77\% of Frame-Ada clips end in the real state according to the two judges.
\textit{L3, errors grow with contact:} the EVID gap to the real video is largest for wipe, turn, slide, and topple, 0.5 to 0.7\,cm, and close to zero for open, flip, and fold.
\textit{L4, short clips of one embodiment:} 3\,s at $256\times320$ per checkpoint.
