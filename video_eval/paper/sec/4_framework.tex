Two references bound every score: the real video of the same actions is the ceiling, and the still video, the first frame held for 16 frames, is the floor, with perfect image quality and no motion.
Cosmos-Predict2.5~\cite{nvidia2025cosmos} enters with actions, post-trained on Bridge, and without actions, given only the first frame and the instruction, which tests whether the action input matters.
\Cref{tab:metrics} lists the metric groups; all are computed per clip, and we call a difference between two models significant when a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test over the clips gives $p<0.05$.
The 2-D track follows the moving CoTracker3 point best explained by the recorded end-effector position, confirmed to lie on the gripper by two vision-language models, and compares its generated and real tracks with $\mathrm{NDTW}=\exp(-\mathrm{DTW}/(16\,d_{\mathrm{th}}))$, $d_{\mathrm{th}}=20$\,px.
