\paragraph{Models and references.}
\Cref{tab:models} lists the inputs and outputs of the models we compare, and \cref{fig:pipeline} shows how each video is scored.
Cosmos-Predict2.5~\cite{nvidia2025cosmos} enters in two versions of the same 2B backbone: the model post-trained on Bridge with actions, and the model without actions, given only the first frame and the instruction text, which tests whether the action input matters.
Two references bound every score: the real video of the same actions is the ceiling, and the still video, the first frame held for 16 frames, is the floor, with perfect image quality and no motion.
Cosmos videos are resampled to the IRASim format of 16 frames at $256\times320$; for the model without actions we keep every third of its 49 frames at 16\,fps, which spans the same 3\,s.

\paragraph{Metric groups.}
\Cref{tab:metrics} lists what each group measures and what it cannot measure.
Every metric is computed per clip, and two models are compared with a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test over the same clips; we call a difference significant when $p<0.05$, that is, when a difference this consistent across clips would arise by chance less than 5\% of the time if the two models were equally good.
The 2-D track score follows the end-effector in the image.
CoTracker3~\cite{karaev2024cotracker3} tracks a $20\times20$ grid of points from frame 0 through the real and the generated video, and the end-effector point is the moving point whose real track is best explained by the recorded end-effector position through an affine camera; two vision-language models confirm that the point lies on the gripper.
NDTW~\cite{ilharco2019ndtw} compares the generated and the real track of that point,
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{NDTW} = \exp\!\left(-\frac{\mathrm{DTW}(\hat x_{0:15}, x_{0:15})}{16\, d_{\mathrm{th}}}\right),
  \label{eq:ndtw}
\end{equation}
where DTW sums the pixel distances between the two tracks after aligning them in time, and $d_{\mathrm{th}} = 20$\,px; NDTW is 1 for identical paths and tends to 0 for unrelated ones.
