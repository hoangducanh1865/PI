\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our region-text contrastive loss aligns object regions with prompts in training without masks at inference, improving the GR-ConvNet + CLIP baseline by 2.5 H points on Grasp-Anything++.
Restricting inference to Grounding DINO boxes or ground-truth masks adds up to 6.1 and 16.2 Unseen points, showing room for stronger grounding.
