\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A training-only region-text contrastive loss improves a GR-ConvNet + CLIP baseline by 2.5 H points over three seeds, yet perfect grounding would add 12 to 16 Unseen points more.
Limitations: masks in training, a small weight, a small Unseen gain, and no part-level grounding in the labels.
