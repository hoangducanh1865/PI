\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

LGD~\cite{vuong2024language} and LLGD~\cite{nguyen2024lightweight} use diffusion and consistency models, GraspMamba~\cite{nguyen2024graspmamba} state-space models, and GraspSAM~\cite{noh2024graspsam} finds the object with Grounding DINO~\cite{liu2024grounding} before a SAM-based grasp model.
Closest to us, MaskGrasp~\cite{vo2024maskgrasp} uses segmentation features at inference and a triplet loss between grasp-region and mask features.
Our loss instead contrasts image regions with the \emph{prompt} (the InfoNCE loss of CLIP~\cite{radford2021clip} and ALBEF~\cite{li2021albef}, applied to object regions), takes the \emph{other objects of the scene} as negatives, and needs masks only in training.
