\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

LGD~\cite{vuong2024language} and LLGD~\cite{nguyen2024lightweight} use diffusion and consistency models, GraspMamba~\cite{nguyen2024graspmamba} state-space models, and GraspSAM~\cite{noh2024graspsam} finds the object with Grounding DINO~\cite{liu2024grounding} before a SAM-based grasp model.
To resolve wrong-object failures without extra inference modules, our loss contrasts image regions with the \emph{prompt}, takes the \emph{other objects and the background of the scene} as negatives, and needs masks only in training.
