\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Setup.}
We evaluate on Grasp-Anything++~\cite{vuong2024language} with 10,130 training, 515 Seen, and 212 Unseen test objects, totaling 28.6k, 1,376, and 601 image-prompt samples.
We follow the standard training settings~\cite{vuong2024language}: Adam, learning rate $10^{-3}$, batch size 8, 50 epochs of 1,000 batches, selecting the checkpoint on the Seen test set.
A grasp succeeds if its IoU exceeds 25\% and its angle error is under $30^{\circ}$ with opening width scaled by 150; H is the harmonic mean.
We set $\lambda = 0.01$, tuned among $0.01, 0.03, 0.1$ on Seen, and $\tau = 0.07$, reporting mean $\pm$ std over seeds 123 to 125.

\paragraph{Reproduction details.}
In the authors' codebase~\cite{vuong2024language}, the evaluation loader feeds the object category name rather than the full grasp instruction read by our models, and its GR-ConvNet + CLIP detaches visual features before fusion.
Its default training data are drawn from the Seen test objects, so for a fair comparison we score models reproduced with it on Unseen only.

\paragraph{Results.}
With $\lambda = 0.01$, our loss improves the baseline by 3.4 Seen, 1.1 Unseen, and 2.5 H points, with a positive H gain in every seed in \cref{tab:main}; on all 13.5k Unseen prompts of the Grasp-Anything list, it gains 0.9 points across all seeds.
Larger weights are less stable: $0.375 \pm 0.054$ H for $\lambda = 0.03$, and one seed collapses for $\lambda = 0.1$.
Reproduced with the authors' codebase, GR-ConvNet + CLIP reaches 0.18 Unseen, matching the reported value, while LGD reaches $0.29 \pm 0.03$ on 168 random Unseen prompts.
In \cref{tab:gates}, the oracle mask gate adds 12.6 Unseen points to the baseline and 16.2 to ours, and Grounding DINO adds 5.1 and 6.1, showing that contrastive alignment and spatial localization combine.
