EVID: Reference-free Evaluation of Action Fidelity in Videos from Robot World Models via Inverse Dynamics

Duc Anh Hoang

PaperCode

Video world models are compared mostly by how their frames look. EVID reads the end-effector trajectory back from a generated video with an inverse dynamics model and measures how far it deviates from the commanded actions, and whether a real WidowX 250 arm could execute it, without any real video.

Results viewer

Bridge clips generated from the same first frame and actions. Arrows give the better direction. EVID cycle error: distance between the end-effector path read back from the video by the Cosmos3-Nano inverse dynamics model and the commanded path; it needs no real video.

Executable: every pose read back has a WidowX 250 inverse-kinematics solution within the joint limits and every step stays under the velocity limit (1 = yes). The real video row is the reader's own error (its floor); the still first frame is the floor of motion.
