\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A failure analysis of a reproduced baseline showed that language-driven grasp detection on Grasp-Anything++ fails mostly by grasping the wrong object.
A region-text contrastive loss that uses the other objects of the scene as negatives targets this error at no inference cost.
