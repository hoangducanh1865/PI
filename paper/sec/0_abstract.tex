\begin{abstract}
About 47\% of the failures of a reproduced GR-ConvNet + CLIP baseline on Grasp-Anything++ grasp another object than the one in the prompt.
We add a training-only region-text contrastive loss: the encoder features of the prompted object, pooled over its mask, must match the prompt better than the other objects of the same scene.
Under identical training, it improves the baseline by 2.0 points on Seen and 7.5 points on Unseen classes, mostly through fewer missed predictions; wrong-object grasps decrease only slightly.
\end{abstract}
